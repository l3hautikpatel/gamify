📖 How to Play

The Goal

Mafia is a social deduction game. The Mafia win by eliminating the village until they hold the majority. The Village wins by finding and voting out all Mafia members.

Phases

  • Night: The Mafia secretly pick someone to eliminate. The Doctor can save someone, and the Investigator can inspect a player.
  • Day: Everyone discusses who they think the Mafia is.
  • Voting: Players vote to eliminate a suspect. The player with the most votes is removed from the game.

Roles

🤵 Mafia

Coordinate with other Mafia to eliminate villagers at night. Blend in during the day.

👨‍⚕️ Doctor

Choose one person each night to protect from the Mafia's attack.

🕵️ Investigator

Investigate one person each night to learn if they are Mafia or Village.

🌾 Villager

Use deduction and discussion during the day to root out the Mafia.
